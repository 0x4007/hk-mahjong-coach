# Curriculum

The local curriculum progresses from tile literacy through turn flow, melds, legal calls,
three-faan planning, tile efficiency, call discipline, table reading, and full social play. Each
stage names its concepts and suggested unlock evidence without hard-blocking the learner.

The bundled drill library covers tile names and search, sorting, chows, melds, waits, visible-copy
counting, faan, win legality, discard choice, call/pass, relative risk, replay quizzes, and social
table procedure. A transparent mastery update records independent attempts, hint-weighted score,
confidence, and an algorithm version. Due and weak concepts are selected first, then scheduled with
spaced-repetition intervals.
